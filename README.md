## 👋 About Me

I'm a **Python & AI Developer at Bol7 Technologies**, where I build the backend behind an omnichannel communication platform — AI voice calls, WhatsApp Business API, RCS and SMS — and lead **MAYA AI**, an LLM-powered interview panel that generates role-specific questions and scores the answers.

Most of my work lives where models meet production: RAG pipelines that answer from real company documentation, agents that crawl the web and fill forms on their own, and an on-premise **Qwen3** deployment serving internal workloads. Before Bol7 I built a bilingual RAG assistant for **Universal Limited** that let employees troubleshoot a machine component from a photo, in English or Hindi.

I care about the unglamorous half of AI engineering — retrieval that returns the right chunk, queues that don't drop jobs, and deployments that are still running on Monday morning.
